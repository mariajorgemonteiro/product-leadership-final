## 1. Business case

_Why this initiative is worth funding over the alternatives._

| Assumption | Value | Source / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Therapist-matching premium tier drives retention | Unproven | No evidence that therapist access moves retention for our users. This is why it's our hard no this year. |
| Cost of a therapist network | Large, hard to reverse | Building a vetted therapist network is a big cost and liability commitment we can't reverse cheaply. |
| Apple Health integration + content library improve retention | Bet for next quarter | Gathers more data about our users so we can understand them, and tests whether content brings users back to the product. |
| Day-120+ retention of the premium cohort | +10% in 1 year | KR1. Target we'll check before revisiting therapist-matching. |
| 12-month premium renewal among users who converted during their acute phase | +10% in 1 year | KR3. Target we'll check before revisiting therapist-matching. |
| Investment: Growth access to the AI check-in layer | 2 engineers + AI layer access for 6 weeks this quarter | Growth needs the personalised prompt trigger of the AI check-in personalisation layer. |
| Engineering effort for the personalised prompt trigger | ~2 weeks | Can be built in parallel with Core's work on the rest of the AI layer. |
| Day-7 activation with AI layer access | +15% (Q3 OKR) | Expected return if Growth gets the personalised prompt trigger. |
| Day-7 activation without AI layer access (BATNA) | ~+7% | Lower-confidence secondary experiment if Core blocks access until the retention work is done. |
| First delivery of the AI layer to Growth | Within the first month | Agreed with Core: a small first delivery to unblock Growth, then the full layer. |

> **The case in one paragraph:** We will not launch therapist-matching this year. We don't have evidence it moves retention, and building a therapist network is a large, hard-to-reverse commitment. We know this removes a revenue and retention lever the CEO was counting on. Instead, next we'll ship the Apple Health integration and the content library, and analyse whether in one year we reach +10% day-120+ retention of the premium cohort and +10% 12-month premium renewal among users who converted during their acute phase. In parallel, Growth gets an early, split delivery of the AI check-in personalisation layer (about 2 weeks of engineering) to protect the Q3 goal of +15% Day-7 activation instead of ~7% without it.

## 2. Kill criteria

_The specific signals that would tell you this bet is no longer worth pursuing. Be explicit about the metric, the threshold, and the timeline._

> If **day-120+ retention of the premium cohort** does not reach **+10%** by **12 months after launch**, we will **revisit the retention bet before committing more to it**.
>
> If **12-month premium renewal among users who converted during their acute phase** does not reach **+10%** by **12 months after launch**, we will **revisit the retention bet before committing more to it**.
>
> If **data from guided content shows real demand for human support**, we will **bring a scoped therapist-matching pilot proposal to next year's roadmap**.
