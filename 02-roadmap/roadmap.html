From getting through it to staying well

Fable is the wellbeing app people keep paying for after they're already better. We turn each user's private history of what helped them into readiness for what comes next.

How the Rocks were reframed

From features to bets

Crisis mode flow→Playbook captured at relief Apple Watch integration→Passive signals on both platforms Content library→Maintenance paths from existing content

You already win the crisis moment, and a content library is what incumbents do well. Each bet keeps the need behind its Rock but aims it at the part only Fable can own: the user's own history.
