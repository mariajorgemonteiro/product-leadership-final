# Product Strategy One-Pager & OKRs, Module 1

- **Scenario:** Fable Growth (B2C)

## Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| Winning aspiration | Fable is the wellbeing app where people keep paying for after they're already better - not the one that gets you through a crisis and gets abandoned once it worked. |
| Where to play | We focus on young adults 25 to 45 adults to improve their mental wellbeing, who have already gone through an acute stretch and we want to help keep having a good mental wellbeing practices and awareness.
No children or teenagers.
We are a mobile APP and we will not be a desktop APP. |
| How to win | We win because we are expert on resolving a high stress moment. Now we need to help our user to stay away from those stress moments, and teach them techniques to prevent them from being there. Also to continuously monitor their mental health and keep them up-to-date with more recent techniques and studies |
| Capabilities required | we must be world-class on mental health and wellbeing that is part of our users' life.
  - create milestone-based paths (based on the current available content)
 - provide clinical advisory as a safety and escalation review function
- Lifecycle/behavioral personalization infrastructure that can tell where a user sits on the acute-to-stable curve and trigger the right thing at the right life moment |
| Management systems | We track our user retention.

-  user premium cancellation rate
 - conversion from fremium to premium rate
- premium resubscription rate 
 - first milestone-paths completion and second-path re-entry rate tracked per cohort;


And review it monthly |

## One hard no
No clinical treatment, diagnosis, or formal care coordination with providers -  no clinician dashboards, no patient-referral partnerships, no claims of treating or replacing therapy.

## OKR cascade
- **Objective:** Retain users after having a acute stretch  to stay active
- **KR1:** day-120-plus retention of the premium cohort rate increase 10% in 1 year
- **KR2:** post-resolution high stretch return rate 15% in 1 year
- **KR3:** premium cancellations tagged "no longer needed it" reduced by 10% in 1 year
