# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Fable is the wellbeing app where people keep paying for after they're already better - not the one that gets you through a crisis and gets abandoned once it worked.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** We focus on young adults 25 to 45 adults to improve their mental wellbeing, who have already gone through an hard stretch and we want to help keep having a good mental wellbeing practices and awareness.
No children or teenagers.
We are a mobile APP and we will not be a desktop APP.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** We win because we are expert on resolving a high stress moment. Now we need to help our user to stay away from those stress moments, and teach them techniques to prevent them from being there. Also to continuously monitor their mental health and keep them up-to-date with more recent techniques and studies
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** we must be world-class on mental health and wellbeing that is part of our users' life.
  - create milestone-based paths (based on the current available content)
 - provide clinical advisory as a safety and escalation review function
- Lifecycle/behavioral personalization infrastructure that can tell where a user sits on the acute-to-stable curve and trigger the right thing at the right life moment
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** We track our user retention.

-  user premium cancellation rate
 - conversion from fremium to premium rate
- premium resubscription rate 
 - first milestone-paths completion and second-path re-entry rate tracked per cohort;

And review it monthly
