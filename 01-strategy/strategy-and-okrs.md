## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not build a community or social layer.

- Safety and liability exposure. A social space for people coming out of distress will surface crisis disclosures in semi-public settings. That calls for moderation, escalation paths and clinical judgement, pulling us toward the clinical positioning we are not allowed to take.

- Trust. Users came to us privately during a vulnerable time. A social layer changes the nature of that relationship.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Retain users after having a acute stretch  to stay active
- **KR1:** day-120-plus retention of the premium cohort rate increase 10% in 1 year
- **KR2:** 50% of retained graduates maintain or improve their wellbeing score between day 90 and day 180.
- **KR3:** Raise 12-month premium renewal among users who converted during their acute phase 10%
